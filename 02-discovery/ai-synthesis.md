# Discovery Synthesis

## Confirmed case inputs

1. Lena is a frequent mobile BNPL user who uses whichever payment option appears at checkout. Her goal is to pay only after deciding to keep an item.
2. Riverty is unavailable where partner merchants do not offer it.
3. Lena's workaround is Klarna, PayPal, or an existing debit or credit card.
4. Riverty must build a direct consumer relationship without undermining its merchant-first model.

## Product opportunity

Phone-wallet provisioning can make an issued Riverty Card usable in more in-store payment moments. The proposition is a hypothesis, not a confirmed customer need.

## Evidence boundaries

- The synthetic figures (69% and 49% M3 retention; 6.31% and 2.19% eligible-to-activated conversion; -3% partner-checkout spend signal) are synthetic planning signals, not observed Riverty performance. Their lineage is described in `hypothesis-and-metrics.md`.
- The 3% partner-checkout signal informs a proposed guardrail; it is not an approved business threshold.
- Pay-after-deciding is a separate, unvalidated need and remains outside the wallet experiment.
