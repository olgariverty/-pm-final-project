# Individual Insights, Decision and Next Steps

## Friction points

- Lena chooses by availability, not preference, so Riverty has little pull beyond partner checkout.
- Riverty is unavailable outside partner checkout, especially in store.
- The prototype exposed a PRD gap: leftover "Step 1 of 2" labels after the step indicator was removed. The core flow worked as specified.

## Aha moment

The card itself is not the value proposition — broader everyday acceptance is. It creates value only if it adds usage without cannibalising partner checkout.

## Key lessons

1. Keep the experiment to one change (access to phone-wallet provisioning) so any effect is attributable.
2. Treat synthetic figures as planning signals, not observed Riverty performance.
3. A prototype validates flow usability only, not real payments, retention, or business impact.

## Decision requested

Approve a controlled friends-and-family beta of phone-wallet provisioning, subject to a feasibility review (which returns to the decision forum) and to wallet-provider, Risk, and Compliance readiness.

## Scale condition

Scale only if M3 card retention reaches the approved lift threshold and partner-checkout spend stays within the approved boundary.

## Before launch

1. Confirm wallet-provider and scheme support.
2. Freeze the cohort definition (Card PM and Risk); validate baseline comparability and sample feasibility.
3. Secure Risk, Compliance, and Customer Service readiness.

## Next steps

1. Validate provisioning in the friends-and-family beta.
2. Run the experiment against the primary metric and the guardrail.
3. Confirm Compliance and wallet dependencies before any live pilot.
