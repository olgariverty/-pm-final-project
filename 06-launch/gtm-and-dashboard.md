# GTM Launch Plan, Riverty business-unit case

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Feature | Phone-wallet provisioning for eligible Riverty cardholders |
| Goal | Engagement |
| Launch tier | M, Targeted |

## Goal & Audience
- **Goal:** Engagement, This feature should deepen usage of Riverty card by extending it to in-store payments.
- **Target audience:** Eligible, activated, loyal frequent Riverty cardholders in Germany with a compatible phone and supported wallet.

## Launch Tier
- **M, Targeted**, The launch targets a defined cardholder segment and requires focused outreach, rather than broad market awareness. This feature can increase card usage, while poor adoption or provisioning failures would limit its value.

## Channels
1. **Owned: Targeted email with the value proposition, eligibility and setup steps.**
2. **Owned: Targeted push notification directing eligible cardholders to the provisioning flow.**
3. **Owned: In Riverty app card banner prompting eligible cardholders to add their Riverty card to their phone wallet.**

## Enablement & Assets
Setup guide, in-app walkthrough, FAQ and support troubleshooting guide.
CS need eligibility rules, response templates, troubleshooting guide, known-error guidance, escalation path.

## Ownership, Budget & Timeline
- **Ownership & budget:** Launch: Riverty Card Product Manager
Technical readiness: Engineering lead
Customer communication: Marketing Manager (lifecycle owner)
Customer Service: Operations lead
Measurement: Data analyst from celonis
Approval: compliance and risk

Budget: No paid media. Potential costs  - wallet provider. Existing Riverty marketing, analytics and CS teams will support the launch using current tools.
resource gap: dedicated capacity to train CS
Asset gaps: e-mail and in app content, setup guide, FAQ, troubleshooting and escalation guides. Performance dashboard.
- **Timeline:** 1. Beta phase - validate provisioning with the friends-and-family cohort
2. Launch - targeted rollout after the experiment passes the primary metric and guardrail
3. Post-launch - monitor adoption, in-store usage, retention,partner-checkout spend, iterate based on results.

## Success Metrics
- **Metrics:** Wallet-provisioning completion rate among eligible cardholders.
Share of provision cardholders making at least 1 instore waller transaction.
M3 card retention.
- **Bad signal to watch for:** High email or push engagement but low provisioning completion indicates setup friction. High provisioning but low instore usage indicates weak customer value. A partner-checkout spend decline above 3% breaches the guardrail.
- **Likely post-launch decision:** Iterate if provisioning completion or instore usage is weak. Double down only if M3 card retention improves  by at least 10 percentage points and partner-checkout spend remains within 3% guardrail.
