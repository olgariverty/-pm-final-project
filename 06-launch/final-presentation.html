Strategic opportunity

Immediate wallet access can extend Riverty beyond partner checkout before the physical card arrives.

Offer one supported phone wallet at approval. Scale only if Month 3 retention improves and partner checkout remains protected.

Slide 6 · Research

Nadine wants fewer payment decisions across online and in-store purchases

Persona and goal

Nadine, 38. A frequent shopper using three or more payment methods.

Current workaround

She relies on memory, then uses Klarna, PayPal or an existing card.

Core friction

Riverty disappears when a merchant does not offer it at checkout.

Simplified journey
1 · APPROVAL
The issued card becomes available
2 · PROVISION
Add to one supported wallet
3 · FIRST USE Core test
Pay without checking merchant support
4 · REPEAT
Measure use again in Month 3
The pilot must answer
  1. Does earlier availability accelerate first use?
  2. Does usage persist into Month 3?
  3. Does partner checkout remain stable?
Boundary

The test covers availability and repeat use. It does not introduce new credit or repayment terms.

Slide 10 · Story

Earlier availability matters only if it creates a durable habit

Aha moment

Immediate wallet access earns investment only when Month 3 usage increases and partner-checkout spend remains stable.

What changed
  1. Nadine's pain and the feature now describe the same acceptance problem.
  2. The primary metric uses data Riverty can observe.
  3. The decision rules protect the merchant-first model.
Next decisions
  1. Validate the cohort, device mix and first wallet.
  2. Confirm the baseline, MDE and achievable sample.
  3. Complete the usability and operational readiness gates.
