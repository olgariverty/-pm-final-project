Slide 5 · Strategy

Riverty disappears when partner checkout does

Problem hook

Lena uses BNPL on most non-trivial purchases but takes whichever provider appears at checkout. She knows Riverty, but rarely seeks it out, so Riverty only reaches her where partner merchants show it. A card could give her a reason to choose Riverty beyond partner checkout.

Moment of misery

Where a partner merchant does not offer Riverty, Lena falls back to Klarna, PayPal, or a bank card.

Value proposition

For Lena, a Riverty card could give her a reason to use Riverty beyond partner checkout.

Why now: Riverty remains dependent on merchant checkout while Klarna and PayPal remain available.

Hypothesis informed by synthetic planning signals

We believe wallet access can increase repeat card use while protecting partner checkout.

Slide 7 · Blueprint

Build only what the experiment needs

  • One supported-wallet flow
  • Experiment instrumentation

  • Second wallet if device mix warrants
  • Guided onboarding
  • Card activity view
Later / separate bets
  • Pay-after-deciding
  • Rewards
  • Broad rollout
PRD must-haves

Eligibility, secure provisioning, success state, actionable recovery, and event tracking.

Scope protection

Credit terms stay identical between arms. No platform promise, immediate-issuance claim, multiple wallets, or promotional nudges during the test.

Planning boundary: Now covers prerequisites plus the test build, not a single-sprint commitment.

Open prototype Validates flow usability only—not real payments, retention, or business impact.

Slide 8 · Validation

Will wallet access create durable usage without cannibalising partner checkout?

Control A

No phone-wallet provisioning during the test.

Variant B

Same experience plus one supported-wallet flow.

Primary + diagnostic

M3: randomised activated cardholders with ≥1 card purchase on days 61–90. In-store: ≥1 card transaction by any method, both arms.

Guardrail

Partner-checkout spend per randomised participant: cumulative days 1–90; zeros included; proposed boundary ≤3% below control. Days 61–90 are a secondary view.
