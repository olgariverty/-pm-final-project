# Final Project Presentation

## Riverty Wallet-Ready Instant Card

**One-line pitch:** Make the Riverty Card available in a supported phone wallet immediately after approval, creating earlier in-store access and sustained card usage without weakening partner checkout.

- Olga Knjazeva

## Executive story

1. **Problem:** Riverty's limited acceptance forces frequent shoppers to switch payment methods and prevents Riverty from becoming an everyday habit.
2. **Persona:** Nadine wants fewer payment decisions across online and in-store purchases.
3. **Solution:** Offer one supported phone wallet immediately after card approval rather than waiting for physical-card arrival.
4. **Validation:** Measure observable Month 3 card retention, supported by provisioning and early-use diagnostics.
5. **Protection:** Pause if partner-checkout spend breaches the proposed guardrail or a material operational risk appears.
6. **Launch:** Begin with a controlled beta, then move to a targeted rollout only if the experiment passes.

## Evidence note

All research references and quantitative figures are synthetic and directional. Baselines, thresholds, sample size, platform, and operational dependencies must be validated before a live launch.
