# Strategic Map

- **Problem hook:** see `problem-hook.md`.
- **Business constraint:** Riverty depends on partner checkout to reach consumers.
- **User friction:** Lena cannot use Riverty where a partner merchant does not offer it.
- **Moment of misery:** Where a partner merchant does not offer Riverty, Lena falls back to Klarna, PayPal, or a bank card.
- **Value proposition:** For Lena, a Riverty card could give her a reason to use Riverty beyond partner checkout.
- **Opportunity:** Let eligible, activated cardholders add their issued Riverty Card to one supported phone wallet for in-store use.
- **Primary behaviour:** Repeat card usage beyond partner checkout.
- **Primary metric:** M3 card retention among randomised activated cardholders (days 61–90 after randomisation).
- **Merchant protection:** Partner-checkout Riverty spend per randomised participant (cumulative days 1–90).
- **Decision:** Scale only if M3 card retention reaches the approved lift threshold and the merchant guardrail holds.

## What this project does not assume

- A specific wallet platform
- Immediate issuance at approval
- A physical-card delivery sequence
- New credit or repayment terms
- That Lena's pay-after-deciding need is solved by wallet provisioning
